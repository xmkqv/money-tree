## notes

### live execution

- live execution is the real dividing line; qlib, vectorbt, pybroker, zipline-reloaded, backtesting.py are backtest-only
- backtesting.py live-trading pr 1009 has been unmerged for three years
- tradingagents, ai-hedge-fund, finrobot are research-grade with no live execution despite 60k-97k stars
- vibe-trading has broker connectors and kill-switches, but its README calls broker trading experimental and not verified against a real account
- llm-agent stars dwarf execution-engine stars and do not track live capability

### licensing

- vectorbt and pybroker ship apache-2.0 plus commons clause, not pure oss; stocksharp is a custom eula
- vectorbtpro is closed-source invite-only and positions nautilus_trader as its live-execution complement
- quantdinger has alpaca and ibkr workflows, but its web and mobile frontends are source-available and require a separate commercial license

### movement

- nautilus shipped bsc and uniswap/pancakeswap dex adapters in 2026-06, moving on-chain
- passivbot v8 breaks v7 configs with no clean migration; requires python 3.12 or 3.14, not 3.13
- octobot 3.0 beta landed 2026-08-06 with a node-based backend and hyperliquid support
- jesse, quantdinger, and hummingbot condor all ship mcp servers; llm-driven research is the 2026 pattern

### ui and substrate

- openalgo is the strongest fully oss dashboard/control plane found, but its securities brokers and market workflows are India-focused
- nautilus_trader ships plotly backtest tear sheets, while its roadmap explicitly excludes live ui dashboards and frontends
- ccxt is the connectivity substrate under freqtrade, hummingbot, octobot, and most crypto bots

### coverage

- dead or archived, still cited by listicles: backtrader (2024-08), ib_insync (archived 2024-03, maintainer died),
  gekko, zenbot, tribeca, ctubio/krypto-trading-bot, tectonicdb, zigma, opentrader (2025-06)
- hftbacktest (4349 stars) and contesttrade (677) went quiet just before the 6-month window; recheck later
- quantrocket has no public repo; free tier excludes live and paper trading
