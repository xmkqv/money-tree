# evidence

[1] UAF 1.3 is a current enterprise-scale RM-ODP analogue.

The April 2026 release defines an IDEAS-grounded metamodel, a viewpoint-by-aspect grid, first-class environment, resource roles, ports, connectors, exchanges, functions, and cross-view traceability.

[UAF 1.3 release](https://www.omg.org/spec/UAF/1.3/About-UAF), [UAF 1.3 domain metamodel](https://www.omg.org/spec/UAF/1.3/DMM/PDF), [unified-architecture-framework](./unified-architecture-framework.md)

[2] SysML v2 has a formal, citable language specification.

OMG document `formal/2026-03-02` carries the formal 2.0 release dated September 2025 and specifies definitions and usages, composite parts, ports, connections, flows, behaviour, views, and viewpoints.

[SysML v2 language specification](https://www.omg.org/spec/SysML/2.0/Language/PDF), [systems-modeling-language-2](./systems-modeling-language-2.md)

[3] SysML v2 entered active certification in June 2026.

OMG launched the SysML v2 certification program on 2 June 2026, providing a current adoption signal for the formal 2.0 specification.

[OMG certification launch](https://www.omg.org/news/releases/pr2026/06-02-26.htm), [systems-modeling-language-2](./systems-modeling-language-2.md)

[4] IEC 81346 remained an actively extended aspect-structuring framework in May 2026.

IEC 81346-14:2026 was published on 19 May 2026 and applies the IEC 81346-1/2 structuring and classification rules to manufacturing and processing systems.

[IEC 81346-14:2026](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/81613), [iec-81346-1](./iec-81346-1.md)

[5] The ISO 15288 process family was extended for systems of systems during May–June 2026.

ISO's May 2026 update lists FDIS 21840, which applies ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288 processes to systems of systems and their constituent systems.

[ISO Update, May 2026, p. 12](https://www.iso.org/files/live/sites/isoorg/files/news/magazine/ISOupdate/EN/2026/iso_update_202605_en.pdf), [ISO/IEC/IEEE FDIS 21840](https://www.iso.org/standard/92475.html), [iso-iec-ieee-15288](./iso-iec-ieee-15288.md)

[6] CESM remains a live compact systems schema.

A study published on 4 April 2026 restates and applies Bunge's composition-environment-structure-mechanism tuple.

[Kesić 2026, §7.2](https://www.mdpi.com/2079-7737/15/7/579#sec7dot2), [Bunge 2004](https://doi.org/10.1177/0048393103262550), [composition-environment-structure-mechanism](./composition-environment-structure-mechanism.md)

[7] ArchiMate's recursive system metaphor received a current Open Group treatment.

The Open Group community guide updated on 26 May 2026 derives constituent systems by Composition and separates Active Structure, Behaviour, Passive Structure, and externally exposed Service.

[ArchiMate 101](https://archimate-community.pages.opengroup.org/workgroups/archimate-101/), [archimate](./archimate.md)

[2026 review](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27399), [Wand and Weber 1990](https://doi.org/10.1109/32.60316), [bunge-wand-weber](./bunge-wand-weber.md)
